3. Rooms and results

Rooms support 2 to 12 participants. The host shares a code and starts when the group is ready. Once play begins, only existing participants can reconnect. A match requires all participants to Like the same title.

The most popular titles at the end of a round without a match are suggestions, not unanimous choices. The host can prepare another round with new titles and broader filters while retaining the room code and group.

Rooms expire after 24 hours. Reconnection preserves participation while possible. A guest leaving the lobby gives up their place; if the host leaves or someone leaves an ongoing game, the game ends for everyone after confirmation. We do not guarantee a particular response time or uninterrupted availability.
